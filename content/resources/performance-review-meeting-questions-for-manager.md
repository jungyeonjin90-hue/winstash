When your manager wraps up reading your performance review out loud and asks, **"Do you have any questions for me?"** it is easy to nod politely and say *"Nope, makes sense—thanks!"*

Saying nothing ends the conversation quickly, but it leaves the most valuable questions unanswered: what actually separates your current rating from the next tier, what stakeholders say behind closed doors, and which Q1 priorities matter most.

Bringing two or three calm, thoughtful questions turns your review meeting from a one-way report card into a real career alignment session.

## Before and after: vague check-ins vs. high-signal review questions

These are illustrative examples. Replace the details with your own work and only use numbers you can back up.

| Weak (produces vague reassurance) | Strong (surfaces actionable clarity) |
| --- | --- |
| Overall, do you think I'm doing okay? | Looking at my work this year, what is the one strength you want me to double down on, and what single habit would raise my impact fastest? |
| When can I get promoted to the next level? | Which competencies of [Next Level] am I already demonstrating reliably, and what are the one or two gaps I need to close in Q1–Q2? |
| Did peers say good things about me? | What themes stood out across peer and partner feedback—especially from teams outside our squad? |
| What should I focus on next quarter? | When leadership plans Q1, which upcoming project or problem would give me the best opportunity to take broader ownership? |

## 16 performance review questions to ask your manager

These are illustrative examples. Replace the details with your own work and only use numbers you can back up.

### 1. Clarifying feedback & unseen perceptions
1. "Across the feedback you collected from peers and partner teams, what surprised you or stood out as a recurring theme?"
2. "Regarding [Growth Area], could you share one or two specific moments from this cycle so I can picture exactly what to change?"
3. "Is there anything I'm spending significant energy on right now that leadership values less than I realize?"
4. "How well is the scope and value of my work visible to directors and peer managers outside our immediate team?"

### 2. Next-level expectations & career trajectory
5. "Looking at our team's rubric or expectations for [Next Level Title], where am I already operating at that bar and where do I still need proof?"
6. "If I wanted to be an obvious candidate for [Next Level / Stretch Role] in six months, what concrete outcomes would we point to by mid-year?"
7. "Are there upcoming cross-team initiatives in H1 where taking lead ownership would stretch the exact skills I'm missing?"
8. "What kind of support, exposure, or mentorship would help me tackle those gaps faster?"

### 3. Next-quarter alignment & team priorities
9. "As we head into Q1, which two or three outcomes for our team matter most to leadership right now?"
10. "Are there recurring bottlenecks or manual processes on the team you'd support me stepping back to simplify?"
11. "Where could I delegate or document current responsibilities so I have capacity for higher-leverage work?"
12. "How should we check in on these goals during regular 1:1s so neither of us waits until mid-year?"

### 4. Manager partnership & communication style
13. "How can I structure my weekly updates or project check-ins to save you time and keep surprises low?"
14. "When risks or slipped dependencies come up on a project, do you prefer a quick Slack heads-up early or a full options memo?"
15. "Are there decisions I currently escalate to you that you'd trust me to make autonomously next quarter?"
16. "How does the timing work for merit or compensation discussions following this review?"

## Copy-and-paste meeting notes & follow-up template

Bring this short agenda into your review meeting doc and fill in the answers while you talk:

```markdown
# Performance Review Discussion Notes — [Date]

## My 3 Priority Questions for Today
1. [Feedback clarity]: What theme from peer/stakeholder feedback should I keep front of mind?
2. [Growth bar]: What 1–2 concrete milestones over Q1–Q2 would show readiness for [Next Level / Stretch Goal]?
3. [Manager rhythm]: How can my updates or risk escalations work even better for you next quarter?

## Key Takeaways Agreed On
- Core strengths to maintain: [Notes]
- Primary 60-day behavior adjustment: [Notes]
- Top Q1 deliverable / stretch project: [Notes]
- Next goal check-in date: [Month/Day in 1:1]
```
